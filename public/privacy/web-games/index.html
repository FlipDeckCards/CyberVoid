Privacy Policy: Deadzone Web Games

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how the browser games on this site that have callsigns, accounts and a leaderboard, such as Mech Invasion and Zombies ("the Games"), handle information. The Games are published by FlipDeck LLC, a company based in California ("we", "us"). The Deadzone Run Android app has its own policy.

Summary

You can play as a guest with just a callsign, or create a free account. We store only your callsign, an optional email address, a hashed password and your scores. We do not run advertising or analytics, and we do not sell your information.

What we store on our server

  • Account (only if you sign up): your callsign, your email address if you give one, and your password stored only as a one-way scrypt hash with a random salt (never in readable form), the date you signed up, and a counter used to sign you out everywhere.
  • Scores: each score you submit: your callsign, score, wave and kills, and the time. A guest score is stored with the callsign you typed, with no account.

We use this to sign you in, to keep your callsign yours, and to run the leaderboard.

What other players can see

The leaderboard shows the top scores with the callsign, score, wave and kills. Do not use your real name as a callsign if you do not want it shown. Your email address and password are never shown.

What stays in your browser

The Games keep small items in your browser's local storage so you stay signed in and remember your callsign: a login token and your callsign (Zombies), and a random session identifier and the email you typed in the optional email box (Mech Invasion). We do not use cookies. Clearing your browser data removes these items. They are not shared with anyone else.

If you type an email in Mech Invasion, the game may send it to our server to look up a saved callsign. The server does not keep an email from that request. It keeps an email only if you create an account with it.

What we do not collect

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking scripts in the Games, and we do not collect your location, contacts or payment information. Our hosting provider may keep standard technical server logs (such as your IP address and the pages requested) as part of running the service.

Where it is stored and who handles it

Account and score data is stored in a database at our hosting provider. We do not sell your personal information or share it for advertising. We may share information if the law requires it, or to protect our rights, or as part of a sale of the business.

Deleting your data

There is no delete button for web game accounts yet. To have your account and scores deleted, email support@flipdeckcards.com from the address on your account (or tell us your callsign) and we will delete it. Guest scores can be removed on request by callsign.

How long we keep it

We keep account and score data for as long as the game and your account exist, or until you ask us to delete it.

Children

The Games are not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change what the Games collect or share, we will update this page. The date at the top shows the latest version.
