Privacy Policy: Deadzone Run

Last updated: October 16, 2026

This policy describes how the Deadzone Run mobile game ("the App") handles information. The App is published by FlipDeck LLC, a company based in California ("we", "us").

Summary

Deadzone Run does not collect, transmit, or share personal information. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no advertising, and no in-app purchases. Your game progress stays on your device.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following data only on your device, using the game engine's local storage (PlayerPrefs):

  • Your best score and best level
  • Your coin balance, shop items, and extra lives
  • Your selected character and your sound and trail settings
  • The player name you type (default "PLAYER")
  • The local top-scores list (name, score, and level)

This data is never sent to us or to anyone else. Uninstalling the App deletes it.

Information we do not collect

The App does not collect or transmit: location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, usage analytics, crash reports from the App itself, or payment information. The App makes no network requests and contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Google Play and Android may report anonymous crash and performance statistics to the developer under Google's own policies. You can manage those in your Android settings.

Permissions

The App requests the Android internet permission, which the game engine adds by default. The App does not use it to send or receive personal data.

Children

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Third parties

The App does not share information with third parties, because it does not collect information.

Changes to this policy

If the App begins to collect or share data, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before the change takes effect.
