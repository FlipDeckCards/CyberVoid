Privacy Policy: Deadzone Run

Last updated: October 17, 2026

This policy describes how the Deadzone Run mobile game ("the App") handles information. The App is published by FlipDeck LLC, a company based in California ("we", "us").

Summary

Deadzone Run has optional accounts, cloud profiles and coin packs bought with Google Play. You can play without signing in, and your progress then stays on your device. If you sign in with Google, we store your account, your cloud profile (best score, level and coin balance), your purchase records and your earned-coin history on our server, so you can keep your progress and coins. There is no advertising.

Information stored on your device

The App saves the following data on your device, using the game engine's local storage (PlayerPrefs):

  • Your best score and best level, and your coin balance, shop items and extra lives (a local copy)
  • Your selected character and your sound and trail settings
  • The player name you type (default "PLAYER")
  • The local top-scores list (name, score, and level)
  • Coin events that have been earned but not yet reported to the server, so they are not lost while you are offline

If you sign in, the App also keeps a session token on your device. The token is stored only in secure storage backed by the Android Keystore. If you do not sign in, this data stays on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes the data stored on your device, but not your account (see "Your choices and deletion").

Information we store on our server

If you sign in, the App sends requests to the Deadzone Run backend (https://deadzone-backend.onrender.com) for sign-in, profile sync, purchases and coin events. The server stores:

  • Account: a link to your Google account (the identifier Google gives us) and your player name.
  • Cloud profile: your best score, best level, selected character, settings and coin balance.
  • Coins and purchases: coin packs are fixed-price and bought through Google Play. We store your coin balance, purchase records with the Google Play purchase reference ID, and your earn history (the coins the App reports as earned in play, each with a reference ID, so the same event is never counted twice).

We do not receive your payment card number or other payment details.

Other information

The App does not collect or transmit: location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone input, advertising identifiers or crash reports from the App itself. The App contains no advertising or analytics SDK.

Google Play and Android may report anonymous crash and performance statistics to the developer under Google's own policies. You can manage those in your Android settings.

Permissions

The App uses the Android internet permission to talk to the Deadzone backend, and to Google for sign-in and purchases. The permissions that Google sign-in and Google Play Billing need are added by those services. The App does not ask for location, contacts, camera, microphone or storage permissions.

Children

The App is not directed to children under 13, and signing in needs a Google account. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Third parties

We do not sell your information. Two Google services handle their own data:

  • Google sign-in: sign-in uses Google's sign-in service. Google's privacy policy applies to that sign-in.
  • Google Play Billing: purchases are handled by Google Play. Google's privacy policy applies to payments.

Our server runs on Render, which hosts the service and database for us.

Your choices and deletion

Note: The retention period and deletion details are pending legal review.

  • In the App: open Settings and choose Delete account. The App lists what will be removed, and deletes the data on your device and your session only after our server confirms the deletion.
  • On the web: the Deadzone site has a deletion page at deadzone-backend.onrender.com/privacy/deadzone-run/delete that explains how to delete your account, including how to ask us by email if you cannot use the App.

What is deleted: your link to your Google account and your player name, your cloud profile, your coin balance and earn history, and your individual purchase records.

What is kept: an anonymous running total of coin-pack sales for each month (which pack, how many, how many coins). It contains no name, account or purchase reference and cannot be traced back to you.

Before you delete: deletion is immediate and cannot be undone. Coins you bought are not refunded when you delete your account. Coin packs are one-time purchases, so there is no subscription to cancel first; deleting your account does not change anything in your Google Play account.

Changes to this policy

If the App begins to collect or share data in new ways, we will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety section before the change takes effect.
